Consider independent objects after overlap separation, space after completion, lettering size and detail complexity. Do not decide from a rigid element-count threshold or from fragments of a generated PNG.
Plan 2 as upper/lower or left/right source regions, or 4 as upper-left, upper-right, lower-left and lower-right regions, according to the source distribution; allow non-equal boundaries. Assign every UI object to exactly one batch. Plan 1 separate complete background image, excluded from the UI count. Respect an explicitly narrower request, such as extracting only a specified object or excluding the background.

Obtain explicit confirmation for that four-image plan before generating any of its UI batches, including when upgrading a 1- or 2-image plan. Reuse an existing explicit confirmation for the same plan; do not treat authorization for 2 as authorization for 4. Authorized 1- or 2-image extraction needs no additional count confirmation.

COMPLETENESS:
Output every requested object as a whole object, including parts slightly hidden by another UI object or cropped by the original image boundary. Reconstruct its missing outline, face, sidewall, corners, rim and decoration by continuing that same object's visible design and light direction. Do not reproduce the shape of the occluder as a notch, hole, missing corner or cut-out. Preserve an intentional opening belonging to the object itself. Do not invent entirely hidden UI objects or guess unknown text or numbers.

OVERLAP SEPARATION:
Objects that appear independently layered or overlapping in the interface must be extracted separately. For example, separate an icon from its button/support, a foreground marker from its illustrated panel, separate stacked books, and separate logo ornaments from the lettered plaque. Restore the complete lower object after removing the upper one. Preserve printed or engraved lettering and surface illustration on their own object; do not split a word into strokes or a material into its paint planes. Do not fuse overlapping objects into one composite silhouette.

TRANSPARENT ARRANGEMENT:
Move the extracted objects apart as needed. Preserve their own shape and proportions rather than their original screen coordinates. Give every complete object a fully transparent margin and separate unrelated objects with actual alpha-zero space; no overlap, touching edges, shadow bridges, clipped edges or shared scenery. Use sufficient canvas space for this planned batch instead of shrinking labels or sacrificing complete silhouettes. Do not add backplates solely to connect floating letters.

REGIONAL BATCHES:
Follow the batch plan decided from the original image before generation: all requested UI in 1 image, upper/lower or left/right in 2 images, or upper-left/upper-right/lower-left/lower-right in 4 images. Regional boundaries need not divide the source equally. This call outputs only one transparent UI image containing this batch's listed objects; do not add other batches, grid panels or dividing lines. An object crossing the regional boundary must be extracted whole in its assigned batch; expand the regional reference or use the full original image for its hidden/context parts. Do not cut an object at the batch boundary or repeat it across outputs.

TEXT AND BACKGROUND:
Follow the stated text policy. When retaining text, preserve the known original words and numbers on their owning UI objects, with readable letterforms; do not rewrite, translate, omit or invent copy. Remove environmental background outside the requested objects. Retain a scene printed inside a UI illustration as part of that illustration. The full environment belongs to a separate background image and must never be included in this transparent UI output.

Output genuine RGBA transparency. No fake checkerboard, opaque background, crop frames, labels, asset names, instructional captions or watermarks. Only the complete, separated, requested UI objects.
